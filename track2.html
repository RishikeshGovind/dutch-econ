The Asymmetric Loss Problem Standard XGBoost minimises symmetric MSE — it penalises over- and under-prediction equally. But understaffing an ICU costs 4–6× more per patient-hour than idle staff. SPO+ trains directly on staffing regret, concentrating prediction accuracy on surge hours.
